About FYM

Students don't just participate.
They build.

FYM started in 2025 to give high school students practical experience through real business and marketing work. Today, it is evolving into a project-first platform where students turn ideas into finished work together.

Why FYM Exists

Students need real responsibility.

Joining an organization should come with a clear way to contribute. FYM is moving toward defined projects, so students can choose work they care about and know what they are responsible for.
